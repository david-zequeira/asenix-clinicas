# Asenix Cinematic Video Prompt Library
## AI Video Assets for Premium Cinematic Websites

> **Purpose:** Create short AI-generated video sequences that can be used inside the Asenix Cinematic Experience Engine.
>
> The objective is NOT to generate generic advertising videos. The objective is to generate **cinematic visual environments** that can later be used as:
>
> - fullscreen backgrounds
> - blurred environmental layers
> - sharp foreground media windows
> - scroll-controlled transitions
> - hero scenes
> - section transitions
> - visual anchors behind typography

---

# 1. Core Creative Principle

Every generated video should behave like a **cinematic website asset**, not like a normal social-media commercial.

The video needs:

- strong composition
- clear focal subject
- controlled camera movement
- clean negative space
- premium lighting
- enough visual continuity for looping
- no unnecessary text
- no logos
- no UI
- no people looking directly into camera unless explicitly requested
- no chaotic camera movement
- no random object transformations

The website will provide the typography, navigation, CTA and interface.

The AI video should provide the **world**.

Use this when creating a new vertical.

```text
Create a premium cinematic website hero video for a high-end digital
experience studio.

SUBJECT:
[describe the vertical and main visual subject]

ENVIRONMENT:
[describe the physical environment]

ARCHITECTURE:
[describe architecture, interior or landscape]

LIGHTING:
sophisticated cinematic lighting, realistic natural light combined with
carefully controlled practical lighting, soft highlights, deep but
natural shadows, subtle atmospheric depth, elegant contrast

CAMERA:
large-format cinema camera aesthetic, shallow depth of field where
appropriate, highly controlled camera movement, precise composition,
slow cinematic push-in / lateral movement / orbit depending on the scene

MOTION:
slow intentional camera movement, smooth stabilized motion, no sudden
movement, no handheld shake, no chaotic motion, no fast commercial cuts

MATERIALS:
physically accurate materials, realistic reflections, refined textures,
premium surfaces, natural imperfections

ATMOSPHERE:
subtle atmospheric depth, realistic volumetric light, controlled haze,
natural environmental movement

COMPOSITION:
strong central focal point, cinematic negative space around the subject,
clean areas suitable for website typography, editorial composition,
balanced visual hierarchy

CINEMATOGRAPHY:
high-end luxury commercial cinematography, sophisticated art direction,
photorealistic, premium production design, subtle film grain, realistic
lens characteristics, restrained color grading

OUTPUT:
8-12 second cinematic shot, seamless visual continuity, no text,
no logos, no watermark, no UI, no subtitles, no artificial interface,
no obvious AI artifacts.

The shot must feel like a frame from an expensive global brand campaign,
not a generic stock video or social media advertisement.
```

# 5. Important Website-Specific Instruction

Add this to almost every prompt:

```text
Designed specifically as a cinematic website background.

Maintain clean negative space around the main subject so large editorial
typography can be overlaid without destroying the composition.

The visual should remain interesting even when partially blurred and
scaled up as a background layer.

The central subject should remain recognizable when displayed sharply
inside a smaller foreground media window.

Avoid important details touching the extreme edges of the frame.
```

This is extremely important for the Asenix effect.

# 28. Generating a Matched Pair

For the Asenix cinematic effect, generate one master shot and use it
twice whenever possible.

```text
MASTER VIDEO
      │
      ├───────────────┐
      ↓               ↓
BACKGROUND         FOREGROUND
blur + scale       sharp + crop
      │               │
      └───────┬───────┘
              ↓
       CINEMATIC EFFECT
```

This creates visual continuity.

If the generator produces a shot with too much detail at the edges,
create a second framing specifically for the background.

# 31. What NOT to Ask the Video Model to Do

Do not ask the model to create:

```text
"Apple-like website"
"modern website"
"futuristic website"
"premium landing page"
"interactive UI"
```

The AI video model should generate the **visual world**, not the website.

Bad:

```text
Create a futuristic website for a yacht company.
```

Good:

```text
Create a cinematic aerial shot of a contemporary superyacht
cruising through the Mediterranean at golden hour...
```

Then Asenix creates the website around it.

This is the prompt to give another AI when asking it to create a new
vertical prompt.

```text
I am creating cinematic websites for Asenix, a premium AI and technology
studio.

I need an AI video-generation prompt for the following vertical:

[VERTICAL]

The video will NOT be used as a normal advertisement.

It will be used inside a cinematic website system where:

1. The video can be displayed fullscreen.
2. The same video can be duplicated into a background layer.
3. The background version will be enlarged, blurred and darkened.
4. A sharp foreground version will appear inside a smaller media window.
5. GSAP ScrollTrigger will animate the foreground window toward fullscreen.
6. Typography and UI will be layered independently.
7. The footage must therefore have strong composition and visual depth.

Create a premium cinematic scene that communicates the vertical through
architecture, materials, atmosphere and controlled cinematography rather
than clichés.

Requirements:

- photorealistic
- premium commercial cinematography
- large-format cinema aesthetic
- realistic materials
- sophisticated lighting
- restrained color grading
- slow intentional camera movement
- strong central focal point
- 25-35% negative space for typography
- visually strong when blurred and enlarged
- recognizable subject in the central 60% of the frame
- 8-12 seconds
- no text
- no logos
- no watermark
- no UI
- no random futuristic elements
- no chaotic camera movement
- no obvious AI artifacts

The final prompt should be optimized for AI video generation and should
describe the camera, environment, lighting, motion, materials,
composition and atmosphere in detail.

The result should feel like a scene from a high-budget global luxury
campaign rather than a stock video.
```

# 36. Quality Control Checklist

Before accepting a generated clip:

### Composition

- [ ] Strong focal point
- [ ] Subject mostly inside central 60%
- [ ] Negative space for typography
- [ ] Works when cropped

### Motion

- [ ] Camera is stable
- [ ] Movement is intentional
- [ ] No sudden changes
- [ ] No morphing
- [ ] No strange physics

### Materials

- [ ] Reflections are coherent
- [ ] Textures remain stable
- [ ] Geometry remains consistent
- [ ] Lighting stays coherent

### Website compatibility

- [ ] Looks good fullscreen
- [ ] Looks good cropped
- [ ] Looks good blurred
- [ ] Can loop or cut cleanly
- [ ] Has a strong poster frame

### Brand quality

- [ ] Does not look like stock footage
- [ ] Does not look like generic AI art
- [ ] Does not rely on cliché luxury symbols
- [ ] Feels premium without being excessive

# 37. The Asenix Rule

Every vertical should answer this question:

> **If all text, UI and branding disappeared, would the video still feel like an expensive digital experience?**

If the answer is no, regenerate the visual.

The website should enhance the video.

The video should not depend on the website to look good.
